The Beijing Normal University Zhuhai Gymnasium is located in Zhuhai, Guangdong Province. Its roof adopts a spatial pipe truss structure with a saddle-shaped plan.

## Structural overview

The maximum structural span is 145 m; the roof panels use concealed-fix BHP high-strength aluminum-zinc plated sheet, covering about 11,000 m².

Project parameters are overall project data; our specific scope of work is subject to the contract.
