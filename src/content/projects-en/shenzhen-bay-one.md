One Shenzhen Bay is located by Shenzhen Bay Houhai Park, with a total floor area of about 358,000 m². It is a large urban complex integrating offices, serviced apartments and a hotel.

## Structural overview

The main structure of One Shenzhen Bay Tower T7 adopts the "reinforced concrete core tube + steel radial beams + steel-reinforced columns" system.
