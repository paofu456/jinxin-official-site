One Shenzhen Bay is located by Shenzhen Bay Houhai Park, with a total floor area of about 358,000 m². It is a large urban complex integrating offices, serviced apartments and a hotel.

## Structural overview

The main structure of Tower T7 adopts a "reinforced-concrete core tube + steel radial beams + steel-reinforced columns" system. The tower is 341.4 m high, with 76 floors above ground and three below, and uses about 33,000 t of steel.

In this hybrid structural form, the reinforced-concrete core provides the main lateral stiffness while the steel members form the floor framing and structural connections around it. The combination allows the tower's concrete core and steel framing to be planned as one structural system.

## What this project demonstrates

A tower at this height requires coordinated vertical transport, component lifting, connection work, survey control and construction sequencing. One Shenzhen Bay is presented within GSIN's super-high-rise steel structure projects and provides a reference for complex urban developments that combine offices, serviced apartments and hotel space.
