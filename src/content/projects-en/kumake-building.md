Shenzhen Cumark Tower is a high-rise building constructed with the assembled steel structure light-panel building system. Implemented under the EPC contracting model, it is one of Guangdong Province's first prefabricated construction demonstration projects.

## Structural overview

The project is located in Guangming District Technology Industrial Park, Shenzhen, covering a site area of 7,654 m², with a total floor area of about 40,000 m², a floor area of 38,697 m², 2 floors underground and 17 floors above ground. The above-ground part adopts an all-steel structure with about 2,200 tons of steel; the exterior and interior wall panels use autoclaved lightweight concrete (ALC) high-performance concrete panels, the floor decks use steel-bar truss decks, and the curtain wall system adopts an insulating LOW-E energy-saving system.

## Technical background

The project applies the assembled steel structure light-panel building system, jointly developed by GSIN with the China Academy of Building Research, Tsinghua University and Chongqing University. The project is designed to the national three-star green building standard.

Project parameters are overall project data; our specific scope of work is subject to the contract.
