The project is located in Longshan Subdistrict, Longgang District, Shenzhen, bordered by municipal roads on three sides with convenient transportation. The building's main function is to serve as the Longshan transitional business premises during the in-situ reconstruction and expansion of the Longgang Fourth People's Hospital.

## Project overview

The project has a site area of 9,225.70 m², an existing floor area of 7,493 m² and a new floor area of 2,665.43 m². It is a representative Shenzhen steel structure and modular combination building.

Project parameters are overall project data; our specific scope of work is subject to the contract.
