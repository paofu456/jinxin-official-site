Shenwan Huiyun Center is located in the Shenzhen Bay Super Headquarters Base, with a total floor area of about 600,000 m². Sitting in a Shenzhen reclamation area adjacent to three main metro lines, it will become a large urban complex oriented to residence, commerce and offices.

## Structural overview

The Shenwan Huiyun Center Tower J super high-rise consists of a basement, a podium and a tower, with the main structure adopting the "reinforced concrete core tube + steel radial beams + steel-reinforced columns" system.
