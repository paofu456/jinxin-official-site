The Guangming Science City Exhibition Center has a total floor area of 4,390 m² and a 35-day construction period. During construction the project faced high temperatures, rainstorms, typhoons and other weather conditions; the project team kept detailing, fabrication and construction on schedule through 24-hour shift work with 92 machines in operation.

Project parameters are overall project data; our specific scope of work is subject to the contract.
