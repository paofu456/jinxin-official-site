The Guangming Science City Exhibition Center is an assembled steel structure building with a total floor area of 4,390 m² and a 35-day construction period.

## Assembled steel construction

The project is presented within GSIN's assembled steel structure building business. Its short programme depended on coordinated design detailing, factory fabrication and site assembly rather than treating each stage as a separate, sequential activity.

## Delivering to the programme

During construction, the project faced high temperatures, rainstorms, typhoons and other weather conditions. The project team kept detailing, fabrication and construction on schedule through 24-hour shift work with 92 machines in operation. The project moved from wasteland to completion in 35 days.

This case shows how an assembled steel structure can respond to a compact exhibition-building programme and to changing site conditions.
