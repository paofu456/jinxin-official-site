Urban operation building products address small-scale building needs in urban public spaces. With prefabricated steel structures as the main body, the main structure and cladding system can be fabricated in the factory and then transported to the site for rapid assembly.

## Smart prefabricated station (T-BOX Z1)

The current product is the smart prefabricated station, configured with a "public toilet + unmanned retail" functional combination; the specific configuration and delivery scope are determined per project.
