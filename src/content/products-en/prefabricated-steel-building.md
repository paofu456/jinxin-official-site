Prefabricated steel structure buildings combine factory-made steel components with a lightweight building envelope and an organized site-assembly process. GSIN's assembled steel structure light-panel building system brings the structural frame, cladding system and assembly method together as one coordinated building system.

The system was developed on the basis of GSIN's own steel structure fabrication and installation technology. Since 2010, the company has worked with research institutes including the China Academy of Building Research, Tsinghua University and Chongqing University on its development and application.

## What the GSIN system includes

- **Steel-structure body:** the primary structural frame, manufactured for coordinated site assembly.
- **Lightweight cladding system:** exterior and interior building envelope components integrated with the steel frame.
- **Assembled building technology:** standardized design, factory-based production, assembled installation and information-based management brought into one delivery process.

The system can be configured for different building types rather than following one fixed material package. In the Cumark Tower application, ALC high-performance concrete panels were used for the exterior and interior walls, steel-bar truss decks for the floors, and an insulating LOW-E curtain-wall system for the facade. The building was designed to the national three-star green building standard.

## What GSIN delivers

GSIN's business system is organized around professional design, factory-based manufacturing and standardized installation. Depending on the agreed contract, a prefabricated steel building project can bring together structural coordination, component fabrication and site assembly. The specific scope of work is defined for each project.

This approach is intended to connect design decisions with fabrication details and installation sequence before work reaches the site. It also allows the steel frame and lightweight envelope to be planned as parts of the same building system.

## Where prefabricated steel buildings are used

GSIN has applied the system to projects across several building types:

- multi-storey and high-rise buildings
- exhibition and public buildings
- residential projects
- hospital transition facilities and modular combinations

These applications use different layouts, facade systems and project programmes. The common starting point is a coordinated steel structure and building-envelope system, followed by project-specific design and construction planning.

## Representative projects

### Cumark Tower

[Cumark Tower](/en/projects/kumake-building/) is a 17-storey building with about 38,697 m² of floor area and approximately 2,200 tonnes of steel. It was built under the EPC model and is one of Guangdong Province's first prefabricated construction demonstration projects. The project uses the assembled steel structure light-panel building system and was designed to the national three-star green building standard.

### Taizhou Nanyun Jiayuan

[Taizhou Nanyun Jiayuan](/en/projects/taizhou-nanyun-jiayuan/) is an EPC assembled steel structure residential project and a 2021 Jiangsu Province prefabricated construction demonstration project. The building has 7,110 m² of floor area, a height of 36.4 m and 11 floors above ground.

### Guangming Science City Exhibition Center

[Guangming Science City Exhibition Center](/en/projects/guangming-science-city-exhibition-center/) has a total floor area of 4,390 m² and a 35-day construction period. It demonstrates how prefabricated steel construction can respond to a compact public-building programme.

### Longgang Fourth People's Hospital Longshan Transitional Business Premises

[This project](/en/projects/longgang-fourth-hospital/) is a steel structure and modular combination building in Longgang District, Shenzhen. It provides 2,665.43 m² of new floor area as transitional business premises during the hospital's reconstruction and expansion.

Project parameters are overall project data; our specific scope of work is subject to the contract.

## Quality and recognition

Cumark Tower received project recognition including the 2017 Guangdong Steel Structure Gold Award and Shenzhen's first prefabricated steel structure building certification in the housing and construction system. GSIN was recognized as one of Guangdong Province's first prefabricated construction industrial bases and was later recognized as a National Prefabricated Construction Industrial Base. In 2023, the company was recognized as the Guangdong Assembled Steel Structure Light-Panel Building Engineering Technology Research Center.

The company's published intellectual-property record includes 10 software copyrights, 7 invention patents, 49 utility models and 7 registered trademarks.
