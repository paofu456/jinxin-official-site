Steel structure villa residence products use heavy steel as the main frame of the house. The cladding system adopts a composite wall composed of dry-hung ceramic panels and ALC wall panels, with the main body and cladding components fabricated in the factory and then transported to the site for assembly.

## Representative project

- [Qiufu Prefabricated Heavy-Steel Villa](/en/projects/qiufu-steel-frame-villa/): located in Meizhou, Guangdong Province, with heavy steel as the main frame of the house.
